# project-1
Overview

Welcome to the MYSHO Sales Dashboard repository! This project presents an interactive and insightful analysis of MYSHO's sales performance across various dimensions, including region, product category, gender, and order date.
The dashboard is designed to provide quick and actionable insights to stakeholders, helping them monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and identify areas for growth or concern.
Key Features & Metrics
The dashboard focuses on the following key metrics and visualizations:

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

Sum of Profit: $1.23K
Total Orders: 100
Sum of Quantity: 316.00
Sum of Total Sales: $6.14K
Total Revenue: $1.41K (with a goal of $1.41K, showing 0% variance)
Profit: $281.00 (with a goal of $24, showing a significant +1070.83% increase!)
Sales by Region: Breakdown of total sales across East, North, South, and West regions.
Total Sales by Category: Comparison of sales across Electronics, Accessories, Home, Furniture, and Office.
Total Sales by Gender: Distribution of sales between Male and Female customers.
Product ID by Total Sales: A detailed breakdown showing the sales contribution of different products.
Total Sales by Profit: A time-series-like view showing total sales volume across different profit levels.
Total Sales by Order Date: A trend line showing sales volume from Feb 2024 to Apr 2024.

 Key Insights from the Data
 
Based on the dashboard's current view, we can highlight the following:
Exceptional Profit Performance: The project shows a substantial over-performance in the Profit metric, achieving $281.00 against a goal of $24 (a >1000\% increase).
Regional Focus: The West region appears to be the leading contributor to total sales.
Category Sales: Electronics is the top-selling category, followed by Accessories.
Gender Distribution: Sales are slightly higher for Male customers compared to Female customers.
